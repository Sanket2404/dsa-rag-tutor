How RAG Parameters & Guardrails Work

🛡️ What is the "Relevance Guardrail"?

When a user asks a question, ChromaDB computes a Cosine Similarity score (0% to 100%) between the question vector and your indexed documents.

Without a guardrail: If you ask "Who won the 2022 World Cup?" on an AI company manual, the vector DB will still return random unrelated paragraphs (e.g. 45% match). The AI would attempt to answer and hallucinate!

With the guardrail (Default 65%): If the highest matching paragraph is below 65%, the RAG engine immediately blocks the query and replies: "I cannot find relevant information in the uploaded documents."

  • High (75% - 85%): Strict. Only answers if exact, high-confidence paragraphs are found.
  • Balanced (65%): Recommended. Blocks out-of-domain queries while answering valid ones.
  • Low (50% - 60%): Permissive. Answers even with partial or tangential matches.

🌡️ What is "Model Temperature"?

Temperature controls the randomness/creativity of text generation:

  • 0.0 – 0.2 (RAG Gold Standard): Strictly deterministic and fact-bound. Forces the model to stick 100% to the retrieved text.
  • 0.7 – 1.0 (Creative): Introduces varied vocabulary and novel phrasing, but drastically increases hallucination risk.

📦 What is "Top-K Chunks"?

The number of most relevant document snippets pulled from ChromaDB to feed into the prompt context. 3 chunks is usually the sweet spot between sufficient context and fast latency.

🤖 MiniLM vs. Ollama: What is the Difference?

This is the #1 interview question in RAG architecture:

  • MiniLM (all-MiniLM-L6-v2): It is an Embedding Model (384-dimensional vectors). Its only job is to convert text into numbers so ChromaDB can search it. It cannot write sentences or chat.
  • Ollama: It is a Generative LLM Runner (runs models like LLaMA 3.2, Mistral, Gemma locally). Its job is to read the retrieved chunks and write fluent, human-like answers.
  • How they work together: MiniLM does the Retrieval (search), and Ollama does the Generation (writing)!
